Zoom Meeting Integration Guide (Version 0.1)
How to make Rukky appear as a participant in Zoom classes today
💡
Immediate Zero-Cost Solution: In Version 0.1, you can bring Rukky directly into any Zoom meeting using free, industry-standard tools: OBS Studio Virtual Camera and VB-CABLE Virtual Audio Device.
Step 1

Install Free Tools (100% Free & Lightweight)

  • OBS Studio: Free screen and video compositor (obsproject.com)
  • VB-CABLE Virtual Audio Device: Free audio routing driver (vb-audio.com/Cable)
Step 2

Capture Rukky's Video in OBS

  • Click the Stage View button on Rukky's viewport in this application to open the dedicated avatar-only window.
  • In OBS Studio, click Sources (+) → Window Capture, and select the GISSUF AI Avatar window.
  • Crop the capture so only Rukky's animated avatar is framed in your camera scene.
  • In OBS Studio, click Start Virtual Camera.
Step 3

Route Rukky's Voice & Microphone

  • In Windows Sound Settings, set Rukky's browser output to CABLE Input (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).
  • In Zoom Settings → Audio, select CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable) as your Zoom Microphone.
  • This allows everyone in your Zoom training class to hear Rukky speak in crystal-clear audio!
Step 4

Select OBS Virtual Camera in Zoom

  • Open Zoom → Settings → Video.
  • Select OBS Virtual Camera as your Camera.
  • Rukky will now appear in your Zoom meeting with synchronized speaking animations, blinking, and professional GISSUF Technology branding!
